Who We Are

Climate action should belong to everyone.

ClimateBuddy is an environmental initiative bringing individuals, communities and partners together to take practical action against climate change.

  • ✓ Restore forests and degraded ecosystems.
  • ✓ Support communities affected by climate change.
  • ✓ Promote climate education and environmental awareness.
  • ✓ Encourage practical, measurable environmental action.
What We Do

Turning concern into climate action.

We focus on practical environmental projects that communities can participate in and measure.

🌳

Tree Planting

Support tree planting, reforestation and restoration of degraded landscapes.

🌿

Ecosystem Restoration

Help restore habitats and natural ecosystems that support biodiversity and communities.

💧

Water Conservation

Promote responsible water use and community activities that protect water resources.

♻️

Waste Reduction

Encourage recycling, responsible consumption and cleaner communities.

📚

Climate Education

Equip young people and communities with knowledge to understand and respond to climate change.

🤝

Community Resilience

Support locally driven approaches that help communities adapt to environmental challenges.

Our Approach
